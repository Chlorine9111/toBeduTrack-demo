### Mathematics FRQ Scoring Notes
Use AP-style point awards for distinct mathematical acts. A point should correspond to observable work, not to general quality impressions.

#### Point design
- Award separate points for setup, method selection, execution, justification, and interpretation when those acts are distinct in the prompt.
- A final answer by itself should earn at most 1 point unless the task explicitly asks only for a value.
- If a theorem or definition is required, the student must both name it and verify its hypotheses to earn the full justification point.
- Minor arithmetic slips after a correct setup may still earn method credit, but not the final accuracy point.

#### Common scoreable acts
| Skill | What earns the point |
| --- | --- |
| Correct setup | Writes the correct equation, integral, derivative, probability model, or test condition that matches the task |
| Method selection | Chooses the right theorem, procedure, distribution, or representation for the stated situation |
| Accurate execution | Carries out the essential computation or algebraic transformation correctly |
| Justification | States why the method applies, including needed conditions such as continuity, differentiability, distribution assumptions, or parameter meaning |
| Interpretation | Expresses the result in the problem context, including units, sign, or practical meaning when relevant |

#### Partial-credit guidance
- Give method credit when the student's pathway is mathematically valid even if the final arithmetic is off.
- Do not award duplicate points for the same work copied across parts.
- If a later part depends on an earlier wrong value but the later reasoning is internally correct, allow follow-through credit when AP style would normally allow it.
- For graph or table interpretations, require the student to connect the representation to the requested quantity; a bare restatement of numbers is not enough.

#### No-credit patterns
- No credit for citing a theorem without checking its hypotheses.
- No credit for a correct numerical answer with no supporting work when the prompt asks for explanation or justification.
- No credit for notation that obscures what quantity is being computed.
- No credit for contextual interpretation that contradicts the sign, unit, or meaning of the computed result.

Before finalizing:
- Ensure every point can be awarded by locating a specific line of student work.
- Ensure the rubric distinguishes setup, reasoning, and accuracy rather than collapsing them into one vague criterion.
