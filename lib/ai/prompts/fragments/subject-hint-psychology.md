### 学科适配：心理学（AP Psychology）

以下规则覆盖心理学课程，在通用 Block 质量要求基础上追加执行。

**example block**
- Start from a concrete psychological scenario, study summary, observation log, survey result, or experimental finding. Never use a vague setup such as "a person feels something."
- Structure steps as a psychology reasoning chain: "identify the observable behavior or claim -> name the relevant concept or theory -> connect it to a specific study, mechanism, or research finding -> interpret the scenario or data -> note one limitation, competing explanation, or ethical issue when relevant."
- Use precise course language such as neurotransmitter, schema, operant conditioning, confounding variable, correlation, or working memory only when it truly fits the prompt.
- Include specific cues from the scenario: age, setting, stimulus, response pattern, study design, measured variable, or result trend.

**paragraph block**
- Teacher: project a short experiment vignette, case study, scatterplot, or survey table; ask students to identify the target concept before naming it.
- Student: annotate the scenario with behavioral evidence, classify the phenomenon, compare two explanations, or evaluate whether the evidence supports causation.
- The activity must specify what students observe, what variable they track, and what claim they must defend or reject.

**definition block**
- Use a three-layer structure: 1) formal psychological definition 2) a concrete scenario or classic study reference 3) a boundary check that distinguishes the term from a nearby concept.
- When defining a research-method term, state what counts as evidence in that design and what conclusion the design can or cannot support.
- When defining a cognitive, developmental, social, or clinical concept, include an observable behavior or measurable indicator.

**quiz block**
- Base the item on a concrete case, short study description, graph, or research scenario rather than on isolated term recall.
- Target concept application, research-method interpretation, data reading, or theory comparison.
- The explanation must cite the specific cue in the scenario or data that makes the answer correct and briefly explain why the tempting distractors represent common psychology confusions.

**关键原则**
Psychology is only "specific" when it includes a named concept, observable evidence, and a justified interpretation. Avoid pop-psychology language, unsupported diagnosis, and correlation-as-causation leaps.
