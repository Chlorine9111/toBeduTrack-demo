The Plastic
Crisis

An inquiry-based exploration of global waste management systems. Students will investigate the lifecycle of synthetic polymers and design local community interventions to reduce microplastic contamination.

Difficulty

Advanced

Duration

6 Weeks

Subject

Science

Standard

NGSS HS-LS2

The Driving Question

"How can we engineer a circular economy at a municipal level to eliminate plastic waste from our local watershed by 2030?"

Project Phases

04 Key Milestones
01

Launch & Discovery

Activities

  • fiber_manual_record Documentary screening and guest lecture.
  • fiber_manual_record Initial waste-stream audit at home.

Objectives

Identify the scale of global plastic pollution and establish personal relevance through local observation.

02

Chemical Analysis

Activities

  • fiber_manual_record Laboratory polymer identification.
  • fiber_manual_record Degradation rate modeling.

Objectives

Understand the molecular structure of plastics and why biological systems struggle to break them down.

03

Intervention Design

Activities

  • fiber_manual_record Rapid prototyping of filter systems.
  • fiber_manual_record Community stakeholder interviews.

Objectives

Apply engineering design principles to create a scalable solution for a specific local context.

Assessment Rubric

Criteria Exemplary (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2)
Research Depth Extensive data sets and peer-reviewed sources. Multiple reliable sources cited correctly. Limited sourcing or over-reliance on one medium.
Prototype Design Fully functional, tested, and iterative design. Functional prototype with minor flaws. Conceptual model only, no testing data.
Scientific Literacy Advanced mastery of polymer chemistry. Accurate use of chemical terminology. Frequent misuse of scientific terms.
