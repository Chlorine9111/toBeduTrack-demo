The Action Potential

A neural impulse; a brief electrical charge that travels down an axon. Understanding the "All-or-None" response in biological psychology.

AP Psychology Neural Communication Unit 2

Key Mechanisms

bolt Resting Potential

The fluid outside an axon’s membrane has mostly positively charged sodium ions; a resting axon’s fluid interior has a mostly negative charge.

waves Depolarization

The first section of the axon opens its gates, and positively charged sodium ions flood through the membrane channel.

refresh Refractory Period

A period of inactivity after a neuron has fired. Subsequent action potentials cannot occur until the axon returns to resting state.
