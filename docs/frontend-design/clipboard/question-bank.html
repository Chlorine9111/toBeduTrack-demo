#MECH-042
Medium Multiple Choice

A block of mass 2.0 kg is pushed against a spring with spring constant k = 500 N/m, compressing it by 0.1 m. When released, the block slides across a horizontal surface with a coefficient of kinetic friction μ = 0.2. What is the velocity of the block after it has traveled 0.5 m from the equilibrium position?

A 1.25 m/s
B 2.45 m/s check_circle
C 3.12 m/s
D 0.85 m/s
info Explanation

First, calculate the potential energy stored in the spring: PE = ½kx² = ½(500)(0.1)² = 2.5 J. As the block moves, work is done by friction: W = μmgd = 0.2(2.0)(9.8)(0.5) = 1.96 J. By the work-energy theorem, KE_final = PE_initial - W_friction = 2.5 - 1.96 = 0.54 J. Finally, v = sqrt(2KE/m) = sqrt(2 * 0.54 / 2.0) ≈ 2.45 m/s.
