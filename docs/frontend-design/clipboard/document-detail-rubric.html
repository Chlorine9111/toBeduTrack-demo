Cell Structure & Function

This comprehensive rubric evaluates students' understanding of cellular compartmentalization, organelle interactions, and membrane permeability. Designed for AP Biology Unit 2 assessment standards.

Criteria Excellent (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2) Beginning (1)
Organelle Function Clearly articulates the role of all major organelles and how they interact to maintain homeostasis. Identifies all major organelles and correctly describes their primary individual functions. Identifies most organelles but lacks detail in functional description. Limited identification of organelles or incorrect functional descriptions.
Membrane Transport Mastery of active vs. passive transport mechanisms with specific protein examples. Correctly distinguishes between active and passive transport in various scenarios. Inconsistent application of transport principles. Some terminology errors. Confuses active and passive transport mechanisms frequently.
Surface Area to Volume Sophisticated mathematical modeling showing impact on nutrient exchange efficiency. Accurately calculates and explains why high SA:V ratios are essential for cells. Recognizes the concept but struggles with calculating ratios or implications. Shows little understanding of the physical constraints on cell size.
Evolutionary Origins Detailed evidence for Endosymbiosis (DNA, Ribosomes, Membranes) expertly integrated. Explains Endosymbiotic Theory with at least two pieces of supporting evidence. Mentions Endosymbiosis but provides incomplete or vague evidence. Unable to describe the origins of complex eukaryotic organelles.

Notes for Instructors

This rubric is designed for both peer-review and summative assessment. When grading the "Excellent" category for Organelle Function, look for students who can explain the pathway of a secreted protein (ER → Golgi → Vesicle) rather than just listing what each part does.
