Ancient Rome: The Rise of an Empire

Marcus Aurelius Jr.

Senior Curriculum Designer

verified Made with Deskmate
01

Learning Objectives

check_circle

Understand the transition from the Roman Republic to the Roman Empire.

check_circle

Identify the architectural and engineering marvels that defined Roman urbanism.

check_circle

Analyze the social hierarchy and its impact on everyday life in the capital.

02

Main Activity: The Forum Simulation

Students will be divided into four societal groups: Senators, Plebeians, Merchants, and Soldiers. Using provided primary source excerpts, each group must debate a proposed decree for the expansion of the Appian Way.

"The strength of the empire lies not in its walls, but in the consensus of its people." — Teaching Note
