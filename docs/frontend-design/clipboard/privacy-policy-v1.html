Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2024 • Version 2.1

Summary

At The Editorial Workspace, your data is treated like a private manuscript. We collect only what is necessary to curate your digital experience and never trade your focus for profit.

1. Information We Collect

Our collection process is designed to be minimal. We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, subscribe to our newsletter, or communicate with our support team.

Account Data

Email address, full name, and workspace preferences used to authenticate your access.

Usage Patterns

Interaction data that helps us understand which editorial tools are most valuable to your workflow.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information we curate is used strictly to provide, maintain, and improve the services we offer. This includes:

  • check_circle Personalizing your editorial dashboard and curation tools.
  • check_circle Sending technical notices, updates, and security alerts.
  • check_circle Monitoring and analyzing trends to improve user experience.

3. Data Sovereignty

We believe in "Data Sovereignty." Your content belongs to you. You can export your workspace data in standard JSON or Markdown formats at any time without restriction.

4. Third-Party Integration

The Editorial Workspace integrates with certain third-party services to enhance your productivity. When you connect these services, we only request the minimum permissions required to function. We do not sell your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data. This includes TLS encryption for data in transit and AES-256 encryption for data at rest.

Questions about our policy?

Our privacy officer is available to discuss any concerns regarding your data and our handling practices.
